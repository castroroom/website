our training is built on three core pillars:

personal practice

the development of a three-dimensional understanding of the many facets of yoga, as well as the effort to integrate its concepts into personal behaviors and relationships.

seeing others

witnessing, holding space, identifying energetics and aesthetics, and honing the stewardship of an educator.

communication

how we talk about the things we know and love, connecting with other people through shared fascination and honesty, without being didactic or a caricature.
