common questions

Is it okay if I miss a weekend?

Yes. We record all lectures and discussions, so you can listen back if you miss anything, and it's easy to get notes from classmates as well. One of the reasons we use the extended format is that everyone has a life to navigate, and this training makes it especially easy to go deep while remaining flexible. Makeup work depends on what you miss, but the work is generally light and geared to help you understand the material covered.

Does the training come with any free classes?

All teacher training sessions and classes are included in tuition, and all enrolled students get a free 10-pack of classes to use as they wish.

How are the weekends structured?

Each day is different, with a deliberate mix of physical practice, seated lectures, interactive discussions, and all manners restorative and meditative practice. Sessions range from 1-3 hours, with 30-minute breaks between.

What is the capacity?

We cap the training at 36 people, which makes for a spirited but intimate experience.

Is the training Yoga Alliance certified?

Yes. All graduates qualify for a 200RYT designation.
